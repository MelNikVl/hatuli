description: Research Kazakhstan and global real estate competitors for Hatuli and turn verified findings into testable product improvements.

Start with the current Hatuli README and latest improvement log to avoid recommending shipped features. Research first-party sources such as Krisha valuation, Korter, Kazakhstan BNS housing releases, Zillow methodology and Google Search Central. Record source URL, access date, data period and geography. Verify changing claims during the run.
Compare data provenance, house identity, valuation uncertainty, exposure measurement and discoverability. Asking prices are not transaction prices; archived listings are not confirmed sales, supply counts are not demand. Treat competing product claims as claims, not independent proof of accuracy.
Rank proposed improvements by expected user benefit, evidence, implementation cost and measurable acceptance criteria. Connect acquisition work to useful verified public pages and the 1000 daily users objective. Preserve existing product experiments and consent boundaries; research does not authorize unsolicited messages, ad spending or invented testimonials. Save sources, chosen change and rejected assumptions in docs/daily_improvements.md.
